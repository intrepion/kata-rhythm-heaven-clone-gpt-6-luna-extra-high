# Pip's Picking Party

## Play online

[Play Pip’s Picking Party](https://intrepion.github.io/kata-rhythm-heaven-clone-gpt-6-luna-extra-high/) — free, playable in your browser.

A tiny, original browser rhythm game inspired by call-and-response rhythm games. Help Pip pick eight berries over a 16-beat phrase: listen to the count-in, watch for the glow, and press on the beat.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No build step or dependencies are required. Sound is synthesized in the browser after you start a round.

- Press **Space**, **Enter**, or **↑** to pick.
- You can also tap or click the orchard.
- A hit within about 105 ms is **Perfect**; within about 215 ms is **Close**.
- Use the sound button to mute or restore the beat, or `?` for the quick lesson.

The game uses original vector art and synthesized audio. It is a small homage to the playful rhythm-game format, with its own characters, setting, and cues.
